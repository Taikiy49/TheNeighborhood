# The Neighborhood — public listing package

## Experience name
The Neighborhood

## Description
Your new house is lovely. Shame about the neighbors.

🏡 Move in, decorate your home, and show off your favorite finds.
🚪 Lock the door… or discover why you should have.
🚽 Carry off a Golden Toilet, make your escape, and follow the evidence when YOUR stuff disappears.
🚗 Cruise Maple Street in your own compact car.
🐾 Meet Biscuit, your four-legged neighborhood watch.
📦 Deliver parcels for honest cash. Suspicious, we know.
🔎 Follow five tiny clues to uncover the Moonlight Gnome.
🌦️ Explore sunny streets, passing showers, and community events.

Bring a friend. Hide your valuables. Trust absolutely no gnomes.

Early access: the neighborhood is growing. Up to 8 players. Your home is protected while you're offline.
